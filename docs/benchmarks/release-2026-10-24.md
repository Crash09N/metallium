# 1.0 release benchmarks (2026-10-24)

## Conditions

- Apple M3 Max, macOS 27.0.1, AC power, caffeinate, owner away, `gpu-lock.sh --exclusive` (one game at a time).
  Commit d789077 (release-1.0 before the Voxy high-camera merge; that change only touches the empty-Sodium-pass case
  at high camera heights, which these scenes do not hit).
- Odyssey G5, 2560x1440 @ 144 Hz, real fullscreen, VSync off:
  `./gradlew runClientGameTest -Pbench -PbenchWindow=2560x1440 -PbenchDisplay=Odyssey -PbenchFullscreen -PperfCounters <row flags>`.
  All 18 rows report `bench-env: VALID` (focused, not occluded, drawable/framebuffer/display 2560x1440, AC).
- Every row runs Sodium 0.9.2 (`-PwithSodium` or `-PwithIris`, Iris 1.11.6). GL = the same mods on Apple's OpenGL 4.1 (`-PbackendOff`).
- Scenes: `light` (rd 12), `rd32`, `shaders` (rd 12, `-PbenchRd=32` for the rd 32 rows), `voxy` (Voxy 0.2.20-beta-247adf2
  through nativium-gl, vanilla rd 4). Packs: Complementary Reimagined r5.9.3, Photon v1.4, Rethinking Voxels r0.1-beta9;
  default settings. DH row: Distant Horizons + Reimagined.
- **Not comparable with `shootout-2026-10-20-final.md`:** the bench world (seed back to "metallium", 975593a) and the
  camera (above ground again, 72a7bcc) changed after that run; GL Reimagined went from 33 to 80 fps from that alone.
- Columns: rendered fps (avg), shown/s (frames actually presented, Metal surface counters only), frame time p50 / p99,
  1% low fps, Java heap.

## Reading

- Metal is faster than OpenGL in every scene both can run: light +156 %, rd32 +62 %, Reimagined rd12 +22 %, Reimagined
  rd32 +18 %. The 1% lows gain more than the averages (light 6.9x, rd32 4.3x, Reimagined 1.4x), and the p99 frame time
  is 2-4x lower without a pack.
- Run-to-run spread is large: the same Metal row at the start and at the end of the matrix differed by 23 % (light),
  15 % (rd32) and 4 % (Reimagined). Treat
  differences below ~15 % in the no-pack scenes as noise.
- Phase 2 steps (`storeActions`, `uploadHoist`, `qos.render`) on vs off: within that noise (rd32 499.5 vs 513.5,
  Reimagined 98.2 vs 96.1). They cut encoder counts (STATUS, Phase 2) but do not move the frame rate on this machine.
- Voxy LODs under a pack cost about 25 % of Reimagined's rd12 frame rate (98 -> 73 fps). Rethinking Voxels (geometry
  shader emulation) and DH under Reimagined are the slowest rows.
- Without a pack, every scene is capped by the 144 Hz display for what is actually shown (shown/s ~133).
